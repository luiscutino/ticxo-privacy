TicXO Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

This privacy policy explains how TicXO ("the app"), developed by Luis Cutino ("I," "me," or "my"), handles your information. The short version is that TicXO does not collect, store, or share your personal data.

Information I collect

I do not collect any personal information. TicXO has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no crash-reporting tools, and I do not operate any servers that receive your data.

Data stored on your device

TicXO may save game settings, such as difficulty level and preferences, on your device so the app works the way you left it. This data stays on your device, and I cannot access it. Deleting the app removes it.

Local multiplayer (Wi-Fi)

When you play against someone on the same Wi-Fi network, TicXO connects the two devices directly to exchange game moves. iOS may ask for permission to access your local network for this purpose. This connection is used only for gameplay, and no data is sent to me or to any third party.

Online multiplayer (Game Center)

Online play uses Apple's Game Center. When you use Game Center, Apple may provide TicXO with your Game Center player identifier and display name so it can match you with opponents and show who you are playing against. Game moves are exchanged through Apple's services. I do not store or share this information. Your use of Game Center is governed by Apple's privacy policy, available at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

Diagnostics from Apple

If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may provide me with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. This information does not identify you, and I use it only to fix problems in the app.

Children's privacy

TicXO does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the app collects no personal data, it can be used by players of all ages.

Third parties

I do not sell, rent, or share any information with third parties.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will update it on this page and revise the effective date above.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact me at luis@luiscutino.com.

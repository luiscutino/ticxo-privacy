Common questions

How do I play against a friend nearby?
Make sure both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network and that TicXO is allowed to access your local network (Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network).

How do I play online?
Online games use Apple's Game Center. Sign in to Game Center in the Settings app, then choose online play in TicXO.

Can I play without internet?
Yes. Play against the computer at any difficulty with no connection needed.
